📊 Breath Test Analysis

Best for: Any breath test — a simple lab report with just baseline and peak, or a complete time-point curve for research/detailed analysis. The same form handles both: enter 2 points for a quick read, or the full 0–180 min curve for deeper pattern detection.

Step-by-Step:

1️⃣ Enter Patient Info
Fill in Patient ID (optional), Age, Sex, and Substrate type used in the breath test. This helps the app tailor its analysis.

2️⃣ Add Time-Point Measurements
Click "+ Add Time Point" and enter each measurement from your lab report, or click "🧪 Load Example Curve" to see a worked example:
• Time: Minutes (0, 20, 40, 60, 80, 100, 120, 140, 160, 180)
• H₂: Hydrogen reading at that time
• CH₄: Methane reading at that time

3️⃣ Enter As Many Points As You Have
The more data points you enter, the deeper the analysis:
• Minimum: 2 points (e.g. baseline at 0 min + your peak reading) — enough for a diagnosis
• Recommended: 8-10 time points — unlocks curve-shape insights like sustained-elevation checks
• Ideal: Full 180-minute test with measurements every 20 minutes

4️⃣ Select Symptoms (Optional)
Check any symptoms you've experienced — Bloating, Pain, Diarrhea, Constipation, Gas, Nausea. This helps correlate symptoms with test results.

5️⃣ Click "Run Intelligent Analysis"
The app calculates rises, analyzes the curve shape if enough points are given, and provides a diagnosis with smart suggestions.

📖 Clinical Interpretation Guide

🧬 Understanding SIBO

Small Intestinal Bacterial Overgrowth (SIBO) occurs when excessive bacteria colonize the small intestine. Diagnostic threshold: H₂ rise ≥20 ppm within 90 minutes. Associated with malabsorption, bloating, and diarrhea in hydrogen-dominant cases.

🟠 Understanding IMO

Intestinal Methanogen Overgrowth (IMO) involves excess methane-producing archaea. Diagnostic threshold: CH₄ ≥10 ppm at any point. Typically causes constipation and severe bloating. May coexist with SIBO.

📊 Pattern Recognition

Early Rise (≤60 min): Suggests rapid transit or proximal overgrowth
Late Rise (>120 min): Suggests slower transit or distal involvement
Biphasic Rise: May indicate multiple sites or mixed presentation
Borderline Values: Require clinical correlation; not definitively abnormal

⚙️ Quality Factors

High Baseline CH₄ (≥10 ppm): Affects interpretation; suggests baseline methanogen colonization
Incomplete Test: Results <180 min may miss delayed rises
Rapid Transit: Early rise may indicate transit issue rather than SIBO
Substrate Type: Lactulose vs glucose affects sensitivity/specificity

💊 Treatment Considerations

SIBO (H₂-dominant): Rifaxomicin, low-FODMAP diet, prokinetics
IMO (CH₄-dominant): Antimicrobial therapy, strict dietary restriction
Combined (SIBO+IMO): Combination therapy, extended treatment, close monitoring
Borderline: Symptomatic management, dietary trial, retest in 6-8 weeks
